Privacy Policy

Vintage Wiki: Antique ID · Effective date: October 20, 2026

Vintage Wiki: Antique ID ("the App") helps you identify vintage and antique items from photos. This policy explains what information the App handles and why. The App is operated by Ying Wang ("we", "us").

Information we collect

Photos you submit. When you ask the App to identify an item, the photos you choose (and any note you add) are uploaded to our server so the identification can be performed and saved to your collection.

A device identifier. The App generates and uses a random identifier provided by iOS for your device (Identifier for Vendor). It lets the App associate your result cards, your free daily allowance, and your purchases with your device. The App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, or password.

Purchase information. If you buy a subscription or an unlock, Apple processes the payment. We receive confirmation of the purchase (such as the product purchased and a transaction identifier) so the App can unlock the features you paid for. We never see or store your payment card details.

How we use this information

We use the information above only to operate the App: to identify your items, to show your collection history, to enforce the free daily limit, and to unlock premium features. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of processors to run the App:

  • Google Cloud hosts our server and stores your submitted photos and result cards.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that analyzes your photos. Your photos and notes are sent to Anthropic's API for identification and are handled under Anthropic's API data policies; they are not used to train Anthropic's models.
  • Apple distributes the App and processes all payments through the App Store and StoreKit.

Tracking

The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Your photos and result cards are stored so you can revisit your collection. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing ying.wang102@icloud.com and telling us the User ID shown in the App's Settings tab; we will delete the associated photos and cards.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
