Gem Match: Midas Touch · Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Gem Match: Midas Touch (the "App") is developed by Ying Wang. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. We collect no data ourselves

The App has no accounts and no servers. Your game progress, collection and settings are stored only on your device. We do not collect, upload, or sell any personal information; deleting the App removes everything.

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA and device ID) and ad interaction data, and use them for third-party advertising under Google's own policies. On first launch you will see Apple's tracking permission prompt; declining does not affect gameplay — ads are simply less personalized. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses advertising data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
