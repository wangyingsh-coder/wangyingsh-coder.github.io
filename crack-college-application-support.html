Your step-by-step companion for deadlines, FAFSA, the Common App, and financial aid.

Crack College Application helps families stay on top of the three things that matter most in application season: never miss a deadline, never fill a form wrong, never leave aid money on the table. It includes a deadline engine and multi-school progress tracker, step-by-step walkthroughs for FAFSA, the CSS Profile, the Common App and state aid, materials checklists, an aid guide (federal loans and repayment, early vs. regular decision strategy, the community-college 2+2 path), and 234 school cards with official data.

This app is a planning and form-preparation tool. It does not choose schools for you, write essays, or predict admission chances. One-time purchase unlocks everything — no subscription.

FAQ

I bought Full Access. How do I restore it?

Full Access is a one-time purchase. If you switch devices or reinstall and the app shows as locked, use “Restore Purchase” inside the app. If it still doesn’t unlock, email us with a screenshot of your purchase receipt and we’ll help.

Does the app need internet? Is my information collected?

No. The app runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded — the schools, deadlines, and checklist progress you enter stay on your device only.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Where does the school card data come from?

Admission rates, international student share, SAT / ACT ranges, and popular majors on each school card come from the school’s officially published Common Data Set and are labeled with their data year. Where official data could not be found, the card says “To be verified” — we never estimate.

Do deadlines and form rules change?

Yes, details shift a little every application cycle, and the app’s content is updated per season. Always treat each college’s and each system’s official site as the final word — the in-app checklists are preparation reminders, not a substitute for official instructions.
