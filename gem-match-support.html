常见问题

游戏进度会丢吗？
进度保存在设备本地。删除 App 会一并清除进度，请勿随意卸载。

怎么切换中英文？
首次启动跟随系统语言；之后可在 App 内「我的/设置」里随时切换。

广告可以关吗？
看广告是自愿的：续命、奖励翻倍、每日奖励都需要您主动点击观看；横幅与偶尔的插屏是免费版的运营方式，感谢理解。

跟踪授权弹窗是什么？
那是苹果的 App Tracking Transparency 授权：同意后广告更贴合您的兴趣；拒绝完全不影响游玩，广告只是没那么个性化。

Gem Match: Midas Touch · Support

Gem Match: Midas Touch is developed by Ying Wang. A match-3 puzzle game built around real gemstones. Progress is stored locally on your device; no account is required.

FAQ

Will I lose my progress?
Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes it, so please don't uninstall casually.

How do I switch between English and Chinese?
The app follows your device language on first launch; you can switch anytime in Settings inside the app.

Can I turn off ads?
Rewarded ads are always your choice — revives, double rewards and the daily bonus only play when you tap. Banners and occasional interstitials keep the game free; thank you for understanding.

What is the tracking permission prompt?
That is Apple's App Tracking Transparency request. Allowing it makes ads more relevant; declining changes nothing about gameplay — ads are simply less personalized.
