Vintage Wiki: Antique ID

Snap a photo of a vintage or antique item and learn what it is — its era, origin, story, and a reference price range, plus a seller's report with an evidence chain, comparable sales, and risk notes.

Support

Need help, or want your data deleted? Email us at ying.wang102@icloud.com. For data deletion, include the User ID shown in the app's Settings tab.

How it works

Take up to 4 photos of your item (a shot of any maker's mark or backstamp helps a lot), pick a category, and submit. Free accounts can identify up to 3 items per day and see the first part of every result card.

Premium

Premium Annual ($39.99/year, with a 7-day free trial) gives you unlimited identifications and the full seller's report for every item. You can also unlock a single item's full report for $1.99. Subscriptions are managed or cancelled anytime in your iPhone's Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, use "Restore Purchases" in the app's Settings tab to restore your subscription.
