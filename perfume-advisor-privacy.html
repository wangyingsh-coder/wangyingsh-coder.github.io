Perfume Advisor · Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Perfume Advisor (the "App") is developed by Ying Wang. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

The App runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts and no servers. We do not collect, upload, or sell any personal information. Your quiz answers and favorites are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

Fragrance detail pages link to official brand websites. Once you leave the App, those websites handle information under their own privacy policies.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
